REVIEW · Gather your key concepts

Use this immediately before the interview.

Opening

“I recommend holding list prices, prioritizing SOTA latency, and piloting enterprise GPT-6 commitments that reward consumption above expected organic growth.”

Decision requested

Approve next-quarter pilots and assign Engineering, GTM and Finance owners. At quarter-end, scale only initiatives that demonstrate incremental consumption and contribution.

Four levers

Hold current prices. A 20% cut assumes 14% more usage, below the 25% revenue and 33% contribution breakevens.
Discount only incremental enterprise GPT-6 allowances above organic spend. Keep one-year expiry and PAYG overage.
Prioritize SOTA. The selected 10% adopter uplift and 5% cost premium produces about +$51M standalone contribution.
Test in parallel. Expand only if it beats the latency-plus-commitment plan; modeled combined breakeven is approximately 5.12% usage uplift.

What would change your mind?

Knowing when to change your mind is a strength.
  • Weaker incremental consumption or acceptance.
  • Higher effective serving cost or ongoing resource need.
  • Greater overlap between product benefits.
  • Insufficient capacity or degraded customer experience.

Closing

“Approve the pilots and owners now. At quarter-end, I would return with observed economics and recommend where to scale, revise or stop.”

Concept collection

Revenue vs. signed commitment

A commitment is a contractual promise. Cash receipt and revenue recognition have different timing. This model forecasts usage revenue and does not assume breakage income.

Contribution vs. margin

Contribution is revenue less serving cost in dollars. Compute margin divides that contribution by revenue. Implementation is deducted separately in the decision metric.

Standalone vs. combined

Standalone tests switch other incremental levers off. Combined results include interactions and overlap. Do not add standalone outputs.

Eligibility vs. adoption

50% eligibility × 80% adoption = 40% of SOTA usage covered at full rollout. A 10% uplift on covered usage implies roughly 4% SOTA uplift before interactions.
